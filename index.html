FROM HARVEST TO CUP

The NOIR Ritual

We treat every step as an art form. Here is how your daily sanctuary comes to life.

01

Highland Harvest

Direct-trade single-origin Arabica sustainably handpicked from the lush volcanic highlands of Aceh Gayo and Toraja, supporting generational farming families.

02

Micro-Batch Roasting

Roasted weekly in small batches on our cast-iron drum roaster. We tailor each batch profile to accentuate cocoa, floral honey, and toasted nutty sweetness.

03

9-Bar Precision Extraction

Pre-infused and pulled at calibrated temperature and 9-bar pressure to yield an exceptionally dense, golden-tiger crema bursting with aromatic oils.

04

The Artful Pour

Steamed to velvety microfoam texture at 65°C, or layered with pure cold-pressed Indonesian aren nectar over slow-melting crystal clear ice.
